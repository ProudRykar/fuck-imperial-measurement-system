# 💥 fuck-imperial-measurement-system

### Tired of inches? Triggered by gallons? Enraged by Fahrenheit?

**We get it.**

This tiny browser extension is your daily act of rebellion against the ridiculous imperial system. Select any cursed unit like `3 inches`, `12 troy ounces`, or even `102°F`, and boom — **a tooltip pops up with metric sanity**.

No buttons. No drama. Just highlight the madness and let the metric healing begin.

---

## 📏 What does it convert?

Everything imperial and unholy:

* 🦶 Length: inches, feet, miles, furlongs (yes, even those)
* ⚖️ Mass: pounds, ounces, troy weights, grains, stones
* 🌡 Temperature: Fahrenheit to Celsius
* 💨 Pressure: inches of mercury to mmHg and Pascals
* 🧪 Volume: gallons, pints, barrels, minims (who even uses those?)
* 🕰 Time: fortnights. Yes, seriously.

---

## 🧠 Why?

Because "12 inches = 1 foot, 3 feet = 1 yard, 1760 yards = 1 mile" is a sentence that should never exist.

1. Open any webpage.
